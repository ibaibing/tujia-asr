## tujia-ctc v0.2 — toned 708-class model

Syllable-level speech recognition for Tujia (Northern dialect) **with tone marks**.
Emits toned IPA syllables with time spans, e.g. `la35`, `tsʰi53`, `ɕiɛ35`.

- `wav2vec2-xlsr-53-espeak-cv-ft` fine-tuned (top 4 of 12 layers trainable) + LayerNorm
  + 2× BiLSTM(256) + Linear(512→709); a 4-class tone auxiliary CTC head is used during
  training only
- 709 labels: 708 toned syllables + blank; tones `21 / 35 / 53 / 55`
- 4,570,826 trainable parameters (18 MB); best epoch 18, validation SER 0.5527
- Strict SER: dev 0.6646 / test 0.4767 greedy, test 0.4634 with syllable-bigram rescoring
  (α = 1.5, tuned on dev only)
- Syllable recall under the lenient protocol: 83.93% on 108 held-out clips, 89.90% over all 685
- Tone accuracy: 65.59% held-out, 80.64% over all 685

### Encoder

The fine-tuned encoder (~1.2 GB) is distributed separately as the Hugging Face model
`xbaibing/tujia-xlsr53-ft`, mirrored on ModelScope as `bbingx/tujia-xlsr53-ft`;
`fetch_encoder.sh` downloads it. It **cannot** be replaced by the original
`facebook/wav2vec2-xlsr-53-espeak-cv-ft` weights: the features differ and recognition
degrades substantially.

### λ_tone ablation

Re-running the identical recipe with `lambda_tone = 0` gives validation SER 0.5561
(epoch 15) against 0.5527 (epoch 18), and tone SER 0.4601 against 0.4204. Over all 685
clips, held-out recall is 82.80% against 83.93%, and the train/held-out gap narrows
from 8.43 to 7.10 points with the auxiliary loss. Keep it.

### Known limitations

Over-segmentation on slow or noisy speech; single-source in-domain data; roughly one
tone in three wrong on test; 43/65-clip dev/test split means differences under ~1 point
are noise; the best epoch was selected on the same 108 clips used in the dev/test table.

See `docs/protocol.md`. Do not compare v0.1's 80.26% recall with the SER numbers above —
they are different protocols.
